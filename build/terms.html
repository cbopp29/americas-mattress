Program Description

America's Mattress Albuquerque operates an SMS notification program to keep customers informed about their mattress and furniture deliveries. Messages include delivery confirmations, driver location updates, and delivery completion notices.

Opt-In

Customers opt in to receive SMS messages by providing their phone number at the point of sale. By providing your number, you agree to receive delivery-related text messages from America's Mattress Albuquerque.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. You will typically receive 2–4 messages per delivery: a confirmation, an en-route alert, a tracking link, and a completion notice.

Message & Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe from all future messages. You will receive a confirmation that you have been unsubscribed.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us directly.

Supported Carriers

Supported carriers include AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, Sprint, and most major US carriers. Carrier support may vary.
