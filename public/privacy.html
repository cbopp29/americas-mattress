Your information is used only to process and deliver your order. We do not sell, share, or distribute your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Delivery records and customer signatures are stored securely and retained for business and warranty purposes. Delivery records are kept for a minimum of 90 days. Customer signatures are kept permanently as proof of delivery.
